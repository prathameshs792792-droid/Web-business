About

Built by a specialist who still does the bench work.

Prathamesh Shinde

Cyber security is the spine. Repair, CCTV, and custom PCs are how that spine meets the real world — a clinic NVR on factory defaults, a CA's only GST disk, a gaming tower that should last two GPU generations.
