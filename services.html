Services

Laptop & PC Repair

Boards, batteries, screens, thermals — diagnosed on the bench, quoted before parts.

From a swollen battery to a board that will not POST, we treat the machine as a system. You get a written estimate, OEM or named-equivalent components, and a machine that boots the way it did the week you bought it.

  • No-POST, liquid damage, and power-jack repair
  • Screen, hinge, keyboard, and battery replacement
  • Thermal paste, fan, and dust-out for throttled machines
  • Windows / Linux reinstall with your files kept where possible
From ₹499 diagnosis Most laptops 24–48 hours

  • Phishing, mailbox, and account-takeover response
  • Endpoint cleanup and ransomware triage
  • MFA, password, and backup hygiene for teams
  • Home and office network hardening
From ₹2,999 consult Same-day remote triage when live-incident

Custom Gaming PCs

Built to a frame-rate, not a colourway. Quoted as a parts list you can actually read.

Tell us the games, the monitor, and the budget. We spec a silent, cool, serviceable tower — cable-managed, stress-tested overnight, with thermals, a parts list, and a one-year assembly warranty.

  • Air or AIO cooling sized to the room
  • Overnight stress test with thermals on delivery
  • Upgrade path noted so the next GPU actually fits
From ₹65,000 builds 5–10 days depending on GPU stock

Site survey, proper cable, night vision and motion zones, locked recorder. You leave with a network map, changed defaults, remote-view on your phone, and a five-minute lesson on pulling footage.

  • Site survey, cable, PoE, and clean NVR setup
  • Night vision, motion zones, and storage math
  • Default passwords killed; remote view hardened
  • Staff training to export a clip without us
From ₹8,999 starter kits Most homes in a day; shops 1–2 days
